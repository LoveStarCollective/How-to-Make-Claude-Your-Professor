# How-to-Make-Claude-Your-Professor
A prompt-engineering case study in directing an AI tutor


So, I decided to have AI teach me machine learning and coding from zero. Turns out
that's not as simple as saying "AI, teach me this ___." You have to teach it how to
teach *you* first, and honestly? That's the whole skill.

This is a case study on what I learned directing an AI tutor: trusting my own logic
even on stuff I knew nothing about, catching it when it's confidently wrong, building
a communication contract, and figuring out that half the flaws I kept blaming on the
machine were really just me not being clear yet.
